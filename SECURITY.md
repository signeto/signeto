# Security

Please report vulnerabilities privately to **security@signeto.dev**. Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We aim to acknowledge reports within two business days and to keep you informed until a fix ships. We will credit you in the release notes unless you ask us not to.

In scope: this repository's packages, the protocol as specified in `docs/protocol.md`, and the Signeto service at `*.signeto.dev`. Out of scope: denial-of-service by volume, social engineering, and findings that require a compromised approver device.
